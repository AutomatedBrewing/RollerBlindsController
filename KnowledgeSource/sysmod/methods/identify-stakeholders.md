# Purpose

It is decisive for the success of the project that the concerns of all Stakeholders are sufficiently considered.

# Description

The list of Stakeholders is initially elaborated in a workshop and continually reworked during the project. Typically, you can reuse the list of Stakeholders from a previous project. Further requirements analysis and system architecting may uncover more Stakeholders.

The Stakeholders' concerns are a source for Requirements, and they are a source for needs that are not explicitly stated but are essential and must be considered by the system and the engineering team.

For each Stakeholder, document the name, concerns, and contact information (email, availability) to be able to get in touch with them. To prioritize the long list of Stakeholders, classify the priority and the effort to consider the Stakeholder. That enables a two-dimensional prioritization matrix, assigning different engagement strategies to each quadrant.
