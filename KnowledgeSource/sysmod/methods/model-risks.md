# Purpose

The Risks specify the potential inability to achieve the System Objectives. They are linked to other model elements to provide traceability to and from the Risks.

# Description

Risk Management is an important topic in itself and is only dealt with to a small extent by SYSMOD. This method only specifies the Risks in the model and links them to other model elements, but does not cover the entire Risk Management process.

The System Idea and System Objectives inputs are intended for the initial setup of the Risks. Later, Risks are also derived from the System Architectures.

For lightweight risk management, the SYSMOD modeling of the Risks could be sufficient. If the Risks are managed in an external risk management tool, the Risks in the model are proxies to establish links and traceability to other model elements.
