# Purpose

Anyone who plays a role in the MBSE Methodology and, to a certain extent, also the Stakeholders of the project must know and be able to use the MBSE Methodology.

# Description

People must be familiar with the MBSE Methodology, modeling languages, and modeling tools. You must differentiate between people who must be able to read the models and people who build the models. These are different skills; the reading skill is a subset of the building skill.

In addition to training, consulting services must be provided for projects to quickly resolve issues, remove hurdles, and get feedback. In the beginning, this can be done by external experts. In the long run, internal persons should take over continuous consultation.

The product MBSE Training represents not a single training but all the different training sessions in different formats necessary to deploy MBSE.
