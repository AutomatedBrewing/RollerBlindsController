# SYSMOD Process

* [SYSMOD Analysis Process](analysis-process.md) - The SYSMOD Analysis Process groups the methods that identify and specify the problem space of the system, including stakeholders, requirements, context, use cases, and domain knowledge.
* [SYSMOD Architecture Process](architecture-process.md) - The SYSMOD Architecture Process groups the methods that create the physical architecture of the system, including logical architecture, product architecture, scenarios, and system states.
* [SYSMOD Infrastructure Process](infrastructure-process.md) - The SYSMOD Infrastructure Process groups the methods that establish and maintain the MBSE methodology, modeling environment, and training for an organization or project.
* [SYSMOD MBSE Adoption Process (SMAP)](mbse-adoption-process.md) - The SYSMOD MBSE Adoption Process (SMAP) applies SYSMOD itself to develop and deploy a customized MBSE Methodology for a project or organization, with the MBSE Methodology as the system of interest.
