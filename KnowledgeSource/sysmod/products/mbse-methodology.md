Product 5.1 in SYSMOD. Even experienced systems engineers need guidance and structures for an effective application of MBSE in a complex engineering environment. The MBSE Methodology provides this structure.

# Purpose

Even if you are an experienced systems engineer, you need guidance and structures for an effective application of MBSE in a complex engineering environment.

# Description

The Roles, Methods, and Products are the toolbox. They are related to each other. The Processes define a useful logical execution order of the Methods and guide the Roles. The tools enable the effective implementation of the Methods.

The MBSE Methodology is produced by tailoring a predefined methodology like SYSMOD to the specific needs of a project or organization.
