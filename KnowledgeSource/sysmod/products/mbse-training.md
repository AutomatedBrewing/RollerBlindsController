# Purpose

It is essential that knowledge and skills about the MBSE Methodology are communicated to the engineering teams and Stakeholders.

# Description

The product MBSE Training represents not a single training, but all the different training sessions in different formats necessary to deploy MBSE. Mainly, they are performed to achieve the requested skills of the SYSMOD Roles. But also some Stakeholders of the engineering project must be trained in MBSE.
