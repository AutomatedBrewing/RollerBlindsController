# Purpose

In engineering, we spend much focus and effort on the solution. It is essential not to forget the original problem to be resolved and to assure it is the right problem that is solved.

# Description

The Problem Statement is a clear statement of the primary purpose that the system is intended to solve. It describes the gap between the current state and the desired state. It is a user-centric statement that answers the key questions: Who is having the problem, What is the problem and the context, and Why it matters.

Example: "Engineers must communicate a lot with other people in distributed teams, but would like to reduce the amount of traveling to save time and minimize the impact on the planet environment."

Design Thinking provides several methods to elaborate on a Problem Statement.
