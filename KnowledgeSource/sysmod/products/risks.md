# Purpose

Requirements and architectures contain risks, and risks, in turn, have an impact on Requirements and architectures. It is essential to have the Risks and links to other elements in the model.

# Description

The Risks are linked to other model elements to ensure traceability and not to overlook the Risks. For lightweight risk management, the SYSMOD modeling of the Risks could be sufficient. If the Risks are managed in an external risk management tool, the Risks in the model are proxies to establish links and traceability to other model elements.
