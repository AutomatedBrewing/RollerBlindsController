# Purpose

It is essential to know all Stakeholders to respond to their concerns and to build a system that meets all their Requirements.

# Description

A Stakeholder is a person or an organization who has concerns about the system that can be the source for Requirements. The Stakeholder can have a direct link to the system like the future users, or an indirect link like an authority that has published laws or rules affecting the system.

System Objectives and Requirements have trace relationships to the Stakeholders who are the source of this information (pre-traceability).
