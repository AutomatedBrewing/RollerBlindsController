# SYSMOD Role

* [MBSE Methodologist](mbse-methodologist.md) - The MBSE Methodologist is responsible for the MBSE Methodology, defining and tailoring it for specific projects or organizations, communicating it to stakeholders, and providing training and coaching.
* [Project Manager](project-manager.md) - The Project Manager manages the systems development project and is directly involved in the initial analysis methods for problem identification and system definition.
* [Requirements Engineer](requirements-engineer.md) - The Requirements Engineer is responsible for identifying, describing, and managing the requirements of the system of interest, closely communicating with the System Architect.
* [SME Administrator](sme-administrator.md) - The SME Administrator sets up and maintains the System Modeling Environment (SME), configuring modeling tools, model libraries, configuration management, and connections to other engineering tools.
* [System Architect](system-architect.md) - The System Architect is responsible for the System Architecture and derives the solution space from the problem space, closely communicating with the Requirements Engineer.
* [System Tester](system-tester.md) - The System Tester is responsible for describing and performing the system test cases and for specifying the test architecture, ideally as part of the development team.
* [Systems Engineer](systems-engineer.md) - The Systems Engineer is the generalization of the SYSMOD roles Requirements Engineer, System Architect, and System Tester, representing any person who practices systems engineering.
