Role 6.2 in SYSMOD. The MBSE Methodologist is responsible for the MBSE Methodology.

# Description

The MBSE Methodologist defines the MBSE Methodology and tailors it for the specific project or organization. The Role is responsible for communicating the MBSE Methodology to all relevant Stakeholders. The MBSE Methodologist also provides the documentation, good practices, and tools necessary to apply the methodology.

The Role gets feedback from the projects about their experiences with MBSE and incorporates it into the tailored MBSE Methodology.

The MBSE Methodologist has a close interface to the SME Administrator. Depending on the concrete competencies of both roles, they allocate among themselves the tasks that need to be done.

The MBSE Methodologists are a center of competence of the MBSE Methodology and provide MBSE Trainings and coaching for the organization. The main part of the MBSE Methodologists' capability should be inside the organization; external consultants can be used to get fresh expertise and a critical view from outside.
