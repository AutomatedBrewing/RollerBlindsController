Role 6.4 in SYSMOD. The Requirements Engineer is responsible for identifying, describing, and managing the Requirements of the systems of interest.

# Description

The Requirements Engineer identifies the requirements—retrieves them from the principal or elaborates them together with the Stakeholders. The Requirements Engineer closely communicates with the System Architect.

In SYSMOD, the Systems Engineer is a collective term for Requirements Engineer, System Architect, and System Tester.
