Role 6.6 in SYSMOD. The Systems Engineer is the generalization of the SYSMOD Roles Requirements Engineer, System Architect, and System Tester.

# Description

A systems engineer is "a person who practices systems engineering" whose systems engineering capabilities and experience include sustained practice, specialization, leadership or authority over systems engineering activities. Systems engineering activities may be conducted by any competent person regardless of job title or professional affiliation. [SEBoK]

The Systems Engineer is the generalization of the SYSMOD Roles Requirements Engineer, System Architect, and System Tester. It is a collective term used when referring to the group of engineering roles.
