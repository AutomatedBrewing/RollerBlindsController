Tool 11.11 in SYSMOD. The 6M method is a simple tool of cause and effect analysis. In the context of SYSMOD, it can be used to work out the Problem Statement.

# Purpose

The 6M method supports the elaboration of the Problem Statement by systematically exploring potential causes of the problem from six perspectives.

# Description

The 6M stands for manpower, machinery, materials, methods, measurement, and mother-nature. They are categories covering potential causes leading to a problem. The diagram (called Ishikawa or fishbone diagram) illustrates the relationship between the effect (the problem) and the contributing causes.

The 6M categories support broad thinking about causes in different areas:

1. **Manpower:** Check the pains of the people doing their jobs.
2. **Machinery:** Check the pains of current products involved in the considered scenarios.
3. **Materials:** Check the pains of the used material. Are they used well? Is the supply and disposal easy?
4. **Methods:** Check the pains of workflows.
5. **Mother-nature:** Check causes coming from the environment.
6. **Measurement:** Check causes resulting from measurement issues like imprecise data.

Write down the potential causes for the problem statement at the appropriate branch in the diagram. The 6M method can be combined with the Five Whys method.
