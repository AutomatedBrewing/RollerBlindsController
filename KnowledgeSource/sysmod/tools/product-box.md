Tool 11.8 in SYSMOD. The product box is a little cardboard box that imaginarily contains the system for selling purposes.

# Purpose

The Product Box is a workshop tool to develop the System Idea and System Objectives in a focused, customer-centric way.

# Description

Take an unmarked cardboard box, some flipchart markers in different colors, and turn the box into an eye-catching packaging for your product. A product box is labeled with:

- A logo and name of the product
- The objectives of the product for the customer
- The main features

The limited space on the cardboard box forces you to focus on the essential aspects. The product box tool is an effective working group tool with excellent results in short times, and it makes fun. It focuses on the customer, which is always a good approach.

Note that internal System Objectives like "This product will beat all competitors out of the market" are typically not written on product boxes.
